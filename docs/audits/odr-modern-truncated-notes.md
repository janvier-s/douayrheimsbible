# Modern ODR: gloss notes shorter than their 1609 text

Found in November 2026 by comparing `static/data/odr` (modern spelling) with the 1609 original-spelling notes,
Peters' modernised edition OCR and the Salette facsimile OCR: 79 modern notes stopped part-way (often
mid-sentence) or dropped a following sentence.

All are fixed. Where the Sancta scrape had a modern-spelling rendering of the missing text, it was used,
trimmed to the note's own end (the scrape sometimes runs on into the next gloss or a margin remark). The rest
were modernised from the 1609 note: Ex 4:25 c, 12:40 i, 22:1 a; Ezech 9:6 d, 12:13 c; Isa 44:9 c;
Ps 90:6 i, 90:11 o, 104:34 t, 105:30 v, 118:62 g; Wisd 11:16 g, 17:2 a; Zach 7:3 a.

Two 1609 notes were corrected along the way:
- **deuteronomy 25:4 a**: held the "Mystically, Pastors and Doctors…" passage, which in the print belongs to
  25:10 c. The modern note was already right.
- **ezechiel 12:13 c**: was cut off at "Babylon,"; completed from the scrape.

A re-run of the comparison finds no modern note shorter than three-quarters of its 1609 text.
